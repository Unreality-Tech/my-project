# Ureality

> **Closing the distance, one shared space at a time.**

Ureality is a technology company that connects people across distances by developing modern mixed-reality and spatial computing experiences.

---

## Overview

Our flagship platform is a mixed-reality and spatial computing headset that lets you invite distant friends or colleagues into your own space as lifelike **spatial personas**. Together, you can:

- 🎬 **Watch movies** side by side, as if sharing the same couch
- 🎮 **Play games** with friends who feel truly present
- 🧑‍💼 **Collaborate** on virtual whiteboards in real time
