No. Each one works alone with the Ureality app, but using several together feels the most real.

Can I visit a friend instead of inviting them?

Yes. Choose "Beam over" and you appear in their room as a hologram.

Is it private?

Only people in your circle can invite you or join a session.

Which phones work?

iPhone and Android are both supported.
